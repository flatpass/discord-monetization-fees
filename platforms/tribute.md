# Tribute

A Telegram-native monetization platform. Gates Telegram channels and groups, not Discord.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Website | https://tribute.tg |
| Pricing page | https://tribute.top/pricing (English); the Russian homepage https://tribute.tg states the 10% too |
| Monthly fee | $0 |
| % of each sale | 10% on subscriptions, donations and digital products · 8% on physical products · 20% for creators Tribute designates Special Category |
| Card processing | included: Tribute states the 10% is all-inclusive, covering payment processing and currency conversion |
| Fixed per transaction | not stated |
| Where the money settles | Tribute, then a twice-monthly payout |
| Payout fees | none charged by Tribute; bank and network fees may apply |
| Payout timing / minimum | paid on the 25th and the 10th (or the next business day), covering the 1st–15th and 16th–31st, to a bank card or in crypto. Minimum for bank card payouts is €100 or 3,000 rubles; below that, the balance carries to the next payout date. |
| Other fees | none stated |
| Verified | 2026-10-06 · primary |

Payment limits: minimum €1 or 100 rubles per donation or subscription. The Russian homepage gives a maximum of €3,000 or 300,000 rubles; the English pricing page gives €2,000 for a one-time donation.

## Notes
- **Telegram, not Discord.** Tribute is in this dataset because it ranks against the Discord platforms for the same queries, and a seller comparing them should be able to see that it gates a different chat platform.
- The site at tribute.tg is in Russian; the quote from it is verbatim, with a translation beside it. An English site with a pricing page is at tribute.top.
- Accepted payment methods listed: Visa, Mastercard, Mir, Tether (USDT), TON and BTC.
