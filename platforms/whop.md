# Whop

A marketplace and checkout platform that gates Discord, Telegram and TradingView access behind a paid membership.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Website | https://whop.com |
| Pricing page | https://whop.com/network/pricing |
| Monthly fee | $0. Pay as you go, no setup fee. |
| % of each sale | 3% on sales that run through an automation — Discord, Telegram or TradingView gating. For a Discord seller that is every sale. |
| Card processing | 2.7% + $0.30 domestic · Whop processes its own cards |
| Fixed per transaction | $0.30 |
| Where the money settles | Whop balance, then payout to the seller |
| Payout fees | ACH next-day ~$2.50 · instant 4% + $1 · Venmo / Cash App / crypto 5% + $1 · wire ~$23 |
| Payout timing / minimum | not stated |
| Other fees | +1.5% on international cards · +1% currency conversion · ACH acceptance 1.5% (max $5) · financed sales (Klarna / Afterpay) 15% · optional add-ons: payment orchestration ~0.8%, billing automation ~0.5%, tax handling ~0.5%, each per transaction |
| Verified | 2026-08-02, 3% re-confirmed 2026-08-03 · direct-confirmation |

All-in on a domestic Discord-gated sale: **3% + 2.7% + $0.30 = 5.7% + $0.30**.

## Sources
- https://whop.com/network/pricing — retrieved 2026-08-02 — monthly fee, processing rate and payout fees. No verbatim quote was recorded at retrieval.
- https://docs.whop.com/fees — retrieved 2026-08-02 — surcharges and add-on rates. No verbatim quote was recorded at retrieval.
- The 3% automation fee — confirmed directly by the maintainer on 2026-08-03, and documented by independent 2026 breakdowns (Ruzuku, Group.app, Dodo Payments, WhatPayment).

## Notes
- **The 3% is not on Whop's public pricing page.** It is the reason this row is tiered `direct-confirmation` rather than `primary`. A reader who checks the pricing page will not find it there.
- **The 30% marketplace commission was removed in May 2025.** Discover sales now pay the same rates as direct sales. Guides still quoting 30% are stale.
- A "$10/month Pro plan" appears in one 2026 review and on no Whop page. It is not recorded here.
- Sales accrue to a Whop balance before payout. That step does not exist on a platform where charges settle into the seller's own Stripe account.
