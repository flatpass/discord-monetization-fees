## Sources
- https://www.patreon.com/pricing — retrieved 2026-08-03 — one plan at 10% of creator income, plus processing, conversion and payout fees. No verbatim quote was recorded at retrieval.

## Notes
- **The 8% / 12% plan tiers are retired.** Guides still describing Patreon as "8–12% depending on your plan" are quoting a structure that no longer exists.
- The 10% is a platform fee. Processing, conversion and payout fees are charged separately, so the all-in cost is higher than 10%.
- The processing rate is not recorded here because it was not captured at retrieval. It is not guessed.
