# Patreon

A membership platform for creators. Offers a Discord role connection, but is not Discord-first.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Website | https://www.patreon.com |
| Pricing page | https://www.patreon.com/pricing |
| Monthly fee | $0 |
| % of each sale | 10% of creator income. One plan, not tiers. |

## Sources
- https://www.patreon.com/pricing — retrieved 2026-10-06 — "10% of the income you earn on Patreon" and "Plus payment processing, currency conversion, and payout fees, and applicable taxes."
- https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/11111747095181-Creator-fees-overview — retrieved 2026-10-06 through a render proxy — "Credit card / Apple Pay: Any - 2.9% + $0.30"; "PayPal / Venmo: US - 2.9% + $0.30"; "PayPal / Venmo: Non-US - 3.9% + $0.30"; "Direct Deposit (ACH/Stripe): $0.25 USD per payout"; "PayPal: 1% (minimum of $0.25, capped at $20 USD)"; "Payoneer: $1.00 USD per payout"; "A 2.5% currency conversion fee applies to any payment made in a currency different from your payout currency".

## Notes
- **The 8% / 12% plan tiers are retired.** Guides still describing Patreon as "8–12% depending on your plan" are quoting a structure that no longer exists.
- The 10% is a platform fee. Processing, conversion and payout fees are charged separately, so the all-in cost is higher than 10%.
- The 10% applies to pages published after 2025-08-04: "The standard 10% pricing plan + relevant taxes applies to any creator who publishes their Patreon page after August 4, 2025".
- Legacy plans still run for older pages, at 5% (Founders), 8% (Pro) and 11% (Pro + Merch). Their processing differs too: legacy micropayment rates of 5% + $0.10 (card / Apple Pay, and PayPal US) and 6% + $0.10 (PayPal non-US) apply to payments of $3 or less, with the standard rates above $3; Founders legacy processing is 1.6% + $0.30 on cards and 2.65% + $0.28 on PayPal.
- On iOS, Apple's in-app purchase system applies a 30% App Store fee. Patreon does not charge additional payment processing fees on iOS transactions, but the platform fee still applies. U.S. customers may have the option to complete purchases through Patreon's mobile web checkout instead of Apple's in-app purchase flow.
- Non-USD processing rates are listed per currency on the same article; they were not captured and are not recorded here.
- Patreon processes the payments itself. The money settles with Patreon and is then paid out to the creator, which is why the payout fees above are Patreon's own line rather than a processor's.
- `support.patreon.com` returns 403 to a fetcher and is blocked for this maintainer's browser. The article was read through a render proxy.
