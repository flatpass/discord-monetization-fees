## Notes
- A secondary source read on 2026-08-02 put Circle at roughly 7% on some plans. Circle's own pricing page as read on 2026-08-23 states 2% / 1% / 0.5% on the three published tiers. The page is what this dataset carries; the change is logged in CHANGELOG.
- The pricing page says Stripe's standard processing fee comes on top of the transaction fee, but gives no rate, so the all-in cost of a Circle sale cannot be computed from it.
- The displayed monthly prices are, per the page, based on annual billing. Month-to-month prices are not displayed.
- The Scale tier ($419, 1%) was not on the page as read on 2026-08-23 and was on 2026-10-06.
- Circle hosts the community itself. A seller using it is not gating a Discord server.
