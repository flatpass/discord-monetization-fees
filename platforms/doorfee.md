## Notes
- The published rate and the rate in DoorFee's own comparison content are not the same. This dataset records the published one, from the pricing section and the terms.
- DoorFee's terms state that payments run through Stripe Connect and that payouts go directly to the seller's connected Stripe account.
- DoorFee's terms contain no holds or reserves language, as read on 2026-08-23 and again on 2026-10-06.
- DoorFee's own comparison pages state fee figures for other platforms. None of them are used here; two are cited in METHODOLOGY as the reason competitor content is never a source.
